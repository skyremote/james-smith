I've turned James Smith's business videos into an AI skill
Over the last month, I've been pulling together 256 videos from James Smith's business channel, and I've turned them into a skill we can use to ask questions about our own businesses.

Each video has its own transcript, and I've indexed them so the skill can find the relevant teaching when we ask it something. That means we can keep the original source attached to the answer and go back to see what he actually said.

For example, should we increase our price? Does this offer still need a sales call? And if we've been posting for a year and feel like we've got nothing left to say, what should we look at next?

Those are the kinds of questions I want to put through it, with the actual offer, the numbers and the problem we're dealing with. The more context we give it, the more useful that discussion can be.

I've put the skill on public GitHub, so you can download it and try it yourself. Open the folder in your AI assistant, ask it to read SKILL.md, and then bring it one decision you've been putting off. The README explains how to get started.

It uses James's published teaching as a reference, and the AI applies that to the context we give it. It's an independent project I've built, so keep that distinction in mind when you're reading the answer.

Get the skill:
https://github.com/skyremote/james-smith

Try this: give it your offer, your price and the objection you keep hearing, then ask it to pressure-test increasing your price and cite the videos it used. Ask which parts come from the source and which parts are its own recommendation. Bring the answer back here and tell me what you agreed or disagreed with.
