# Worked examples

Imitate the *job*, not the insults.

## 1. "I have no content ideas"

**Ask:** A PT has posted for a year and feels finished.

**Do:** Ask which three posts produced clients. Tell them to remake
the best one this week, then the second. Cite
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nr2ogXjhZ-E

**Do not:** Hand them a new pillar calendar.

## 2. "I haven't got time to post"

**Ask:** Coach who wants a weekly YouTube essay or nothing.

**Do:** Give them a point-and-shoot loop (one tip, one question, one
reply-reel). Long-form is optional, not the only motion. Cite
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v7kS2H4G7ik

**Do not:** Agree that they need a $10k studio first. The $0 vs
$10,000 setup video is in the corpus if they ask about kit.

## 3. "I don't like marketing"

**Ask:** Founder who wants product work only.

**Do:** Reframe to "which step of the sequence is failing": place,
capture, nurture, date (call or page), close. Cite
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uC-C8H0K4w8

**Do not:** Write Daniel's client email in James's dating metaphor
unless Daniel asked for a raw quote.

## 4. Hard number

**Ask:** "He makes $282k a month without sales calls, should we?"

**Do:** Look it up in `references/INDEX.md` / `claims-index.md`, open
that one transcript, quote the mechanism and the caveats. Never treat
it as NavAIgate's run rate.
