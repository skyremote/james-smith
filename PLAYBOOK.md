# Playbook — James Smith Business (public YouTube)

Corpus is 256 separate files, indexed in `references/INDEX.md` and
`corpus/index.json`. Open the one or two transcripts that match the
topic. Quote James when the quote is load-bearing; write the answer
for Daniel. His register is blunt — keep that in the quote, not in
outbound unless Daniel asks.

## 1. Content is remakes, not novelty

Same 10–15 videos for a decade. When they are “out of ideas”, find the
asset that got **clients** (not views) and remake it better.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nr2ogXjhZ-E

## 2. Short-form is cheap; long-form is unforgiving

Point-and-shoot one tip, ask a question, reply as the next reel. A
YouTube essay can burn two weeks if the algorithm ignores it.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v7kS2H4G7ik

Highest-leverage skill he names is content itself. The hard jump is
zero to one. People who call teaching “too surface” and have zero posts
have not started the work.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SHYp9OZQWXQ

## 3. “I dislike marketing” means the sequence is broken

Place (live / YouTube / webinar) → capture a signal → nurture →
“date” (call **or** landing page) → some close. List subscribers
raised a hand; they are not doing you a favour.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uC-C8H0K4w8

## 4. Sales calls are a marketing failure, not a default

Naval line he uses: you do sales because you failed at marketing.
A funnel that only ends on a call only works with a call. He wants
time in marketing that talks to thousands, not one-to-one closing.
Rare cases still exist; do not invent them — open
*The Strange Death Of 'Sales Calls'…*

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bXPh0aD7dRU
Also: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oXQIBZMa4M8

## 5. Charge from demand; discounts eat take-home

High-ticket-as-identity is something he attacks. His own PT path was
£25–£40/hour, then ~£40/week online, then capacity. “Charging less”
in his long video is a **business-design** argument, not “be cheap
forever.” Read that transcript before you tell someone to drop price.

Discounting: costs and tax barely move; you eat profit. Rates only go
up, and only because of demand — not credentials.

Cite charge-less: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbQdcCzNDeA
Cite discount: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m7LYrmbF2vM

## 6. The real first target is +$2,000/month, not $10k

He treats $10k/month as the same trap as “lose 10kg” — too far to
celebrate. One kilogram ten times. Extra $2k/month is the change that
matters; he posted for years with no following.

Cite $2k: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v3Myanuy80M
Cite $10k coach: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yiGgKvfoTdk

## 7. Small audience can still take money if the offer is specific

Money is not “wait until you are famous.” A specialist with a real
job (his exhaust-shop example) charges because the work is scarce and
trusted, not because they have a million followers.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Kx49G2_PyE
Live coaching on 16k followers:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M9_F-gAAtZM

## 8. Revenue is not profit; ads work in the wrong story

$282,445/month is **mentorship revenue since Dec 2023 (~$5.1m)**, not
take-home. Staff, ATO, software take a chunk. Academy pays the bills;
this is “fun tokens.” Do not hand Daniel or a client that figure as a
target.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cwdUA4k_-6Q

Paid ads: he says B2B (courses/mentorship) is easy mode; B2C
(yoga, greens, cans) is not. Coaches conflate the two. “100k months”
can have ~45k in ads. Ask who pays (person after tax vs business
expense).

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFhqPmZf6a0

## 9. Email is a written sales asset, done fast

He writes the 403k-list email in the video — product/news hook
(Neutonic in Sainsbury’s), not a five-hour committee draft. The $1m
email strategy video is long; grep it, do not summarise from the
title.

Cite live write: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yZH4I5qWWtY
Cite $1m strategy: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8e5MgNgNxBU

## 10. Effort that looks analog beats AI-looking marketing

Prime Day / Amazon: he will take **less margin per can** for
distribution and a visual hook people believe is real. Effort in the
thumbnail (sign, car wrap, lab-tested gummies) is the point. He is
hostile to Claude-written emails that customers can smell.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1aULI7fRnz4

## 11. PT path and $13.6m are one man’s history

*how i made $13.6m as a PT* is 16k+ words of **his** path. Use it as
a case study. Never as a promise for a client or for NavAIgate.

Cite: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ABBBHnh7oVY
